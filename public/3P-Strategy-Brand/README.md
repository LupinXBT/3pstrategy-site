## Usage rules
1. Clear space: keep a margin equal to the radius of one ring on all sides.
2. Minimum sizes: interlocked mark 24px; simplified mark below that; horizontal lockup 120px wide.
3. Backgrounds: navy ink on Cloud White or white only; white ink on Deep Navy. On photography, place the white mark over an area darker than #3A4757.
4. Do not: recolour the rings individually, add gradients or shadows, outline the wordmark, stretch the lockup, or rotate the mark.
5. Text-only contexts: "3P Strategy" — bold "3P", light "Strategy", one word space, no hyphen.
